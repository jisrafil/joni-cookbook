BBQ Ribs

BBQ · Pork · Main Dish

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 3 hr 5 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 racks baby back ribs
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C).
  2. Remove the membrane from the back of the ribs if it is still attached.
  3. Mix brown sugar, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and cayenne.
  4. Pat the ribs dry and rub the seasoning mixture all over both sides.
  5. Wrap the ribs tightly in foil and bake for 2 1/2–3 hours, until very tender.
  6. Carefully unwrap. Brush generously with BBQ sauce and broil about 5 minutes, watching closely, until the sauce caramelizes.
  7. Rest briefly, slice between the bones, and serve.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Removing the membrane makes the ribs easier to eat and lets the seasoning reach the meat better.
  • Open the foil carefully; there will be hot steam and juices inside.
  • Broilers go from caramelized to cremated with absolutely no warning. Stay there and watch them. 😂

Joni's Notes

Tender ribs do not need to fall apart when you look at them. They just need to pull cleanly from the bone when you bite.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate cooked ribs for up to 3–4 days. Freeze tightly wrapped portions for up to 2–3 months. Reheat covered, then uncover and sauce near the end.

Season until your ancestors are content :)
