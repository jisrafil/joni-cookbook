White Chicken Chili

Chicken · Soup · Comfort Food

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 25 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 pounds cooked chicken breast, shredded
  • 2 cans (15 ounces each) white chili beans or cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 can (4 ounces) diced green chiles
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper, optional
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream or half-and-half
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook 3–4 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  2. Add the chicken, beans, green chiles, broth, cumin, oregano, chili powder, cayenne if using, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bring to a simmer and cook 15–20 minutes so the flavors blend.
  4. Reduce the heat to low. Stir in the sour cream, heavy cream, and lime juice. Simmer gently for 5 minutes; do not boil.
  5. Serve with shredded cheese, avocado, tortilla strips, or more sour cream, if desired.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Mash about 1/2 cup of the beans before adding them if you want a naturally thicker chili.
  • Lower the heat before adding dairy so it stays smooth.
  • Add the lime at the end; that little bit of brightness keeps the creamy broth from feeling heavy.

Joni's Notes

Creamy, cozy, and just spicy enough to remind you it is still chili.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate in an airtight container up to 4 days. Freeze up to 2 months; thaw overnight and reheat gently without boiling.
