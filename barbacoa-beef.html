Barbacoa Beef

Main Dish · Beef · Slow Cooker · Mexican

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 8 hr   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds chuck roast
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon cumin
  • 1 tablespoon oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 3/4 cup beef broth
  • 2 bay leaves

Directions

  1. Cut the chuck roast into several large pieces if needed so it fits comfortably in the slow cooker.
  2. Place the beef in the slow cooker. Add garlic, chipotle peppers, cumin, oregano, cloves, salt, pepper, lime juice, apple cider vinegar, beef broth, and bay leaves.
  3. Cover and cook on LOW for 8–10 hours or HIGH for 5–6 hours, until the beef is very tender.
  4. Remove and discard the bay leaves.
  5. Shred the beef with two forks and stir it back into the cooking juices.
  6. Taste and adjust seasoning before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Chuck roast is ideal because the long cooking time gives the connective tissue time to soften.
  • If you want less heat, use fewer chipotle peppers but keep some adobo sauce for smoky flavor.
  • Shred the beef in the juices instead of draining them away; that liquid is doing important work.

Joni's Notes

Tacos, bowls, nachos, leftovers straight from the refrigerator—this beef has options.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate with its juices for up to 4 days. Freeze in meal-size portions with some cooking liquid for up to 2–3 months.
