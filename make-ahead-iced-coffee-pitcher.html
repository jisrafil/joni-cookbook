Make-Ahead Iced Coffee Pitcher

Iced Coffee · Coffee Bases, Syrups & Cold Foam
Prep: 10 min   Yield: 6 drinks

Ingredients

  • 8 cups bold brewed coffee, cooled
  • 6 tablespoons vanilla syrup
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 6 cups ice, for serving

Directions

  1. Brew 8 cups of coffee using the Cuisinart Bold setting and cool completely.
  2. Transfer the coffee to a covered pitcher and refrigerate.
  3. For each drink, combine about 1 cup chilled coffee, 1 tablespoon vanilla syrup, 1/4 cup milk, and 1 cup ice.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Store the coffee base plain and add milk and syrup by the glass so everyone can customize it.

Joni's Notes

The grab-it-and-go pitcher for mornings that start moving before you do.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Refrigerate the plain coffee base for up to 4 days. Add milk and ice only when serving.
