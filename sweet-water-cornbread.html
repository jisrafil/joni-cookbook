Sweet Water Cornbread

Bread · Southern · Side Dish · Skillet

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 15 min   Yield: 6–8 rounds

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cornmeal
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • Oil, for frying or skillet baking

Directions

  1. Combine the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Whisk the egg, buttermilk, and melted butter together, then stir into the dry ingredients until just combined.
  3. Heat a thin layer of oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  4. Spoon portions of batter into the hot skillet, leaving room to turn them.
  5. Cook about 2–3 minutes per side, until golden brown and cooked through.
  6. Drain briefly if needed and serve warm.

JP Tips & Technique

  • The skillet should be hot before the batter goes in so the outside starts crisping immediately.
  • Do not overmix; stir only until you no longer see dry pockets.
  • If the first round browns too fast before the center cooks, lower the heat slightly. The first one is allowed to be the test cornbread—the cook can eat that one.

Joni's Notes

Crisp edges, tender middle, and absolutely meant to be eaten warm.

Storage & Leftovers

Best the same day. Store cooled leftovers airtight up to 2 days and reheat in a toaster oven, oven, or skillet.
