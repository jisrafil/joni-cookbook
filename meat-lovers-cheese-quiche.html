Meat Lovers & Cheese Quiche

Breakfast · Brunch · Quiche

Prep: 45 min   Cook: 40 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 2–4 tablespoons ice water
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup half-and-half
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1/2 cup cooked sausage
  • 1/2 cup chopped ham
  • 1/2 cup cooked crumbled bacon

Directions

  1. Make the crust by mixing flour and salt, cutting in cold butter, and adding ice water 1 tablespoon at a time until the dough holds together. Shape into a disk and chill 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Roll the dough and fit it into a 9-inch pie plate.
  3. Whisk the eggs and half-and-half until smooth. Stir in the cheese, sausage, ham, and bacon.
  4. Pour into the crust and bake 35–40 minutes, until the center is set and the top is golden.
  5. Rest 10–15 minutes before slicing.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Drain and cool all cooked meats before adding them.
  • Taste the meats before adding extra salt; bacon, ham, and cheese bring plenty.
  • Sautéed onions or bell peppers are welcome, but keep additions modest so the filling sets.

Joni's Notes

This is breakfast making sure nobody leaves the table hungry.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered up to 4 days. Freeze cooled slices up to 2 months. Reheat in a 325°F oven.
