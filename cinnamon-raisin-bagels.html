Cinnamon Raisin Bagels

Bread · Breakfast · Bagels

Prep: 2 hr   Cook: 22 min   Yield: 8 bagels

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups warm water (about 110°F)
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 3 1/2 cups bread flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon
  • 3/4 cup raisins
  • 2 quarts water, for water bath
  • 2 tablespoons honey, for water bath

Directions

  1. Mix the warm water, yeast, and brown sugar. Let stand about 10 minutes, until foamy.
  2. Add the bread flour, salt, and cinnamon. Knead 8–10 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
  3. Knead in the raisins near the end so they stay distributed through the dough.
  4. Place in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise about 1 hour, until doubled.
  5. Punch down and divide into 8 pieces. Shape into bagels and let rest 10–15 minutes.
  6. Preheat oven to 425°F. Bring the water and honey to a boil.
  7. Boil each bagel for 1 minute per side, then transfer to a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  8. Bake 20–22 minutes, until golden brown.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Add raisins after the dough has developed some structure so they do not interfere with kneading.
  • If your raisins are very dry, soak them in warm water for about 10 minutes, drain, and pat them very dry before adding.
  • Cinnamon can slightly slow yeast activity, so judge the first rise by the dough doubling rather than staring angrily at the clock.

Joni's Notes

Toasted with butter? Absolutely. Cream cheese? Also yes. We are not choosing sides.

Storage & Leftovers

Store airtight at room temperature up to 2 days, refrigerate up to 5 days, or freeze up to 3 months.
