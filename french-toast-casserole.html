French Toast Casserole

Breakfast · Brunch · Casserole

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 45 min   Chill/Rest: 8 hr   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf French bread, cubed
  • 6 eggs
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted

Directions

  1. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish and spread the cubed French bread evenly in the dish.
  2. Whisk the eggs, whole milk, heavy cream, sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, vanilla, and salt together until well combined.
  3. Pour the custard evenly over the bread. Press the bread gently so the pieces absorb the liquid.
  4. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  5. When ready to bake, preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Drizzle the melted butter over the casserole.
  6. Bake uncovered for 40–45 minutes, until puffed, golden, and set in the center.
  7. Serve warm with syrup or powdered sugar.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Slightly stale bread absorbs custard especially well without dissolving into mush.
  • Press the bread into the custard before refrigerating so dry pieces do not sit on top all night.
  • For a softer casserole, bake closer to 40 minutes; for a more toasted top, let it go a few minutes longer while watching the edges.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3–4 days. Reheat covered in the oven or in individual microwave portions. Freeze baked portions for up to 2 months.
