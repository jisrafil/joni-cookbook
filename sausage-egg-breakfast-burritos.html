Sausage & Egg Breakfast Burritos

Breakfast · Meal Prep

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 15 min   Yield: 6 burritos

Ingredients

  • 6 large flour tortillas
  • 1/2 pound breakfast sausage
  • 6 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 cup salsa
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Directions

  1. Cook the sausage in a skillet over medium heat until browned and cooked through. Drain excess fat.
  2. Melt butter in a separate skillet and scramble the eggs just until set. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Stir the cooked sausage and cheddar cheese into the eggs.
  4. Warm the tortillas briefly so they are flexible. Spoon the egg mixture down the center of each tortilla and add salsa.
  5. Fold in the sides, roll tightly into burritos, and serve warm or wrap for meal prep.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Let the filling cool slightly before wrapping burritos for storage so trapped steam does not make the tortillas soggy.
  • Do not overcook the eggs; they will cook a little more when reheated.
  • For freezing, wrap each burrito individually so you can grab exactly what you need.

Joni's Notes

This is the kind of breakfast that makes a busy morning much less disrespectful. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate wrapped burritos for up to 3–4 days. For longer storage, wrap individually and freeze for up to 2 months. Thaw overnight or reheat from frozen until hot throughout.
