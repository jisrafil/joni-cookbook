Pulled Pork

BBQ · Pork · Slow Cooker

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 8 hr   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 4–5 lb pork shoulder (Boston butt)
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar

Directions

  1. Rub the pork shoulder all over with the brown sugar, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Place the pork in a slow cooker. Add the BBQ sauce and apple cider vinegar around and over the meat.
  3. Cover and cook on LOW for 8–10 hours or HIGH for 4–5 hours, until the pork is very tender and pulls apart easily.
  4. Transfer the pork to a platter and shred with two forks, discarding large pieces of fat.
  5. Return the shredded pork to the slow cooker and mix it with the cooking juices and sauce.
  6. Serve on buns, with coleslaw, or alongside your favorite BBQ sides.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Pork shoulder is supposed to have some fat; that is part of what keeps it tender during the long cook.
  • If the pork still fights you when you try to shred it, it needs more time. Tender pork shoulder should pull apart easily.
  • For less greasy pulled pork, skim excess fat from the cooking liquid before stirring the shredded meat back in.

Joni's Notes

Low-and-slow food. Put it on, let it mind its business, and come back when the whole house smells like dinner.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate with some of the cooking liquid for up to 4 days. Freeze meal-size portions with sauce or juices for up to 2–3 months.
