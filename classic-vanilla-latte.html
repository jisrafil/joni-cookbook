Classic Vanilla Latte

Hot Coffee & Lattes
Prep: 5 min   Yield: 1 drink

Ingredients

  • 1 concentrated coffee shot
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla syrup
  • Milk filled to the LATTE line of the frother
  • Ground cinnamon, optional

Directions

  1. Add the vanilla syrup to a mug and brew the concentrated coffee shot over it.
  2. Fill the frother with milk to the LATTE line and select hot froth.
  3. Pour the frothed milk into the coffee, spoon the foam on top, and garnish with cinnamon if desired.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Use a medium-dark or dark roast so the coffee flavor stays noticeable through the milk.

Joni's Notes

A simple starting latte that is easy to adjust after the first sip.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Best made fresh. Refrigerate leftover brewed coffee separately for up to 4 days.
