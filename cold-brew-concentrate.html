Cold-Brew Concentrate

Coffee Bases, Syrups & Cold Foam
Prep: 15 min   Yield: 8 drinks

Ingredients

  • 2 cups coarsely ground coffee
  • 8 cups cold filtered water

Directions

  1. Combine the coffee grounds and water in a large covered pitcher or jar and stir until all grounds are saturated.
  2. Cover and refrigerate for 12 to 18 hours.
  3. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer lined with a coffee filter or cheesecloth without pressing hard on the grounds.
  4. Use 1 part concentrate with 1 part water or milk for regular iced coffee, or measure it directly into specialty drinks as written.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Coarse grounds strain more cleanly and are less likely to make the concentrate muddy or bitter.

Joni's Notes

Keep this ready and half the coffee-bar work is already done.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Keep tightly covered in the refrigerator and use within 7 days for best flavor. Do not freeze the entire batch; freeze a portion as coffee cubes instead.
