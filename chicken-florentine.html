Chicken Florentine

Chicken · Italian · Comfort Food

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 25 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1/2 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes

Directions

  1. Season chicken with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  2. Cook chicken 6–8 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same skillet, sauté onion until tender. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in heavy cream and stir in Parmesan, thyme, and oregano. Simmer until slightly thickened.
  5. Stir in spinach and sun-dried tomatoes and cook until the spinach wilts.
  6. Return chicken to the skillet, spoon sauce over it, and simmer briefly before serving.

Joni's Notes

Fresh basil is lovely here. Cream cheese can thicken the sauce. Serve with mashed potatoes or crusty bread so none of that sauce gets abandoned.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days. Reheat gently to avoid separating the cream sauce.
