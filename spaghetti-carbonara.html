Spaghetti Carbonara

Pasta · Italian · Main Dish

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 20 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces spaghetti
  • 4 slices bacon or pancetta, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/4 cup chopped parsley (optional)
  • 1/2 cup reserved pasta water, as needed

Directions

  1. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted water according to package directions. Before draining, reserve about 1/2 cup pasta water.
  2. Cook the bacon or pancetta in a large skillet until crisp. Add garlic and cook about 30 seconds.
  3. Whisk the eggs, Parmesan, salt, and black pepper together in a bowl.
  4. Remove the skillet from direct heat. Add the hot drained pasta and toss with the bacon and garlic.
  5. Quickly stir in the egg mixture, tossing constantly so the hot pasta cooks the eggs gently without scrambling them.
  6. Add reserved pasta water a little at a time until the sauce is glossy and creamy.
  7. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan and parsley if desired.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Take the pan off direct heat before adding the egg mixture. Carbonara wants silky eggs, not breakfast scramble hiding in spaghetti.
  • Have the egg-and-cheese mixture ready before the pasta finishes; timing matters here.
  • Reserved pasta water is your rescue button if the sauce gets too tight.

Joni's Notes

This one moves fast at the end. Everybody should already know dinner is ready before you add the eggs. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Best served immediately. Refrigerate leftovers up to 3 days and reheat gently; the sauce will not be quite as silky the second time around.
