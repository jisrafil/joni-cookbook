Red Beans and Rice

Southern · Beans · Rice · Main Dish

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 2 hr 15 min   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 pound dried red beans
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 pound Andouille sausage, sliced into rounds
  • 2 teaspoons Creole seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (or to taste)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 6 cups chicken stock
  • 4 cups cooked white rice
  • Chopped green onions, for garnish

Directions

  1. Rinse beans and discard any shriveled or discolored beans. Soak overnight in plenty of water.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook onion, bell pepper, and celery for about 5 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and cook 1 minute. Add Andouille sausage and brown on both sides.
  4. Stir in Creole seasoning, thyme, bay leaves, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Drain beans and add them to the pot.
  5. Pour in chicken stock, bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer about 2 hours until the beans are tender.
  6. Smash some beans against the side of the pot to thicken the mixture.
  7. Serve over white rice and garnish with green onions.

Joni's Notes

Serve with cornbread. Adjust the cayenne to your household's definition of 'spicy.' Smoked sausage works if Andouille isn't available.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Keep the beans at a gentle simmer so they soften without breaking down too quickly.
  • Smashing a small scoop of beans against the side of the pot gives you that creamy body without adding anything extra.
  • Hold the rice separately if you expect leftovers so it does not absorb all the bean liquid.

Storage & Leftovers

Cool beans and rice promptly. Refrigerate up to 3–4 days; for best texture, store rice separately when practical. Beans freeze very well.
