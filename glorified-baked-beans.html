Glorified Baked Beans

Side Dish · Beans · Cookout

Prep: 30 min   Cook: 1 hr   Yield: 10–12 servings

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces peppered bacon, diced
  • 2 pounds lean ground beef
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 cans (16 ounces each) kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 can (16 ounces) small white beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 cans (16 ounces each) pork and beans
  • 1 cup ketchup
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons white vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoons dark molasses
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • Salt, to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 300°F.
  2. In a large deep skillet or Dutch oven, cook the bacon until crisp. Transfer to paper towels if needed.
  3. Add the ground beef and onion to the pan. Cook until the beef is browned and the onion is soft, then drain excess fat.
  4. Return the bacon to the pan and stir in all of the beans.
  5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the ketchup, brown sugar, vinegar, Dijon mustard, molasses, honey, and salt.
  6. Pour the sauce over the bean mixture and stir until evenly coated.
  7. Transfer to a 9 × 13-inch baking dish. Bake uncovered for 1 hour, until thick and bubbly.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Use any combination of sturdy canned beans you enjoy; keep the pork and beans for the saucy base.
  • Drain the meat well so the finished beans stay rich instead of greasy.
  • They thicken as they sit, so let them rest 10 minutes before serving.

Joni's Notes

These are not the quiet little beans sitting beside the plate. These beans showed up dressed for the cookout.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate in an airtight container up to 4 days. Freeze up to 3 months. Thaw overnight and reheat covered, adding a splash of water if needed.
