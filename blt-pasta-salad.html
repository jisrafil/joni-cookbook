BLT Pasta Salad

Salad · Pasta · Side Dish

Prep: 25 min   Cook: 10 min   Chill/Rest: 1 hr   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces rotini pasta
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 6 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup Duke's mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1 tablespoon white vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 cup chopped lettuce (optional)

Directions

  1. Cook the rotini in salted water until al dente. Drain, rinse briefly with cool water, and let it cool completely.
  2. Whisk the Duke's mayonnaise, sour cream, white vinegar, salt, and black pepper together.
  3. Combine the cooled pasta, tomatoes, bacon, cheddar, and dressing in a large bowl.
  4. If using lettuce, fold it in just before serving so it stays crisp.
  5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Cool the pasta before adding the mayonnaise dressing so it stays creamy rather than oily.
  • Save a little bacon for the top if you want it to stay especially crisp.
  • If making this ahead, hold the lettuce until serving time.

Joni's Notes

Yes, the mayonnaise is Duke's. We have established house rules. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate up to 2 days for best texture. Do not freeze. If adding lettuce, add only to the portion you plan to serve.
