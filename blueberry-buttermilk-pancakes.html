Blueberry Buttermilk Pancakes

Breakfast · Brunch

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 15 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries
  • Butter or oil, for cooking

Directions

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl.
  2. In a second bowl, whisk the buttermilk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined. A few small lumps are fine.
  4. Gently fold in the blueberries so you do not crush them.
  5. Heat a skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly grease with butter or oil.
  6. Pour about 1/4 cup batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form on top and the edges begin to look set, then flip and cook the second side until golden.
  7. Serve warm with syrup.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Do not overmix the batter; overmixing develops gluten and makes pancakes tougher instead of tender and fluffy.
  • If using frozen blueberries, add them straight from the freezer so they bleed less into the batter.
  • Keep finished pancakes warm on a sheet pan in a low oven while you cook the rest.

Joni's Notes

Breakfast doesn't need to be complicated to feel special. A stack of blueberry pancakes and everybody suddenly acts like it's a holiday. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate cooled pancakes in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days. Reheat in the toaster, skillet, or microwave. Freeze with parchment between pancakes for up to 2 months.
