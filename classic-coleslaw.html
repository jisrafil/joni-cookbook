Classic Coleslaw

Side Dish · Salad · Southern

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 0 minutes   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 small head green cabbage, finely shredded
  • 2 carrots, peeled and shredded
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 2/3 cup Duke's mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon celery seed
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Combine cabbage, carrots, onion, green onions, and red bell pepper in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk Duke's mayonnaise, apple cider vinegar, sugar, and celery seed in a separate bowl. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Pour dressing over the vegetables and toss until well coated.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  5. Toss again before serving.

Joni's Notes

Best chilled. Add vinegar if you like it tangier. This belongs next to BBQ or fried chicken and I will hear no arguments.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate in a covered container and use within 3–4 days. It will soften as it sits. Do not freeze.
