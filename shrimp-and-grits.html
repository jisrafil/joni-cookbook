Shrimp and Grits

Seafood · Southern · Comfort Food

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 30 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup stone-ground grits
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt (for shrimp)
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 slices bacon
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 2 green onions, chopped

Directions

  1. Bring water to a boil, stir in grits and salt, and reduce heat. Simmer 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Stir butter and cheddar cheese into the grits.
  3. Season shrimp with salt and pepper.
  4. Cook bacon in a skillet until crisp. Remove and crumble.
  5. Sauté onion, bell pepper, and garlic in the bacon drippings. Add chicken broth and simmer.
  6. Add shrimp and cook just until opaque and cooked through.
  7. Spoon shrimp mixture over grits and garnish with green onions and crumbled bacon.

Joni's Notes

Hot sauce is welcome. Smoked sausage can change things up. Do not bully the shrimp by cooking them into little rubber commas.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate promptly and use within 3 days. Store grits and shrimp separately when possible. Reheat grits with a splash of water or milk; warm shrimp gently.
