Hazelnut Coffee Creamer

Homemade Coffee Creamers
Prep: 5 min   Yield: About 1 cup

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup half-and-half
  • 1/8 to 1/4 teaspoon hazelnut extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon pure vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Combine the condensed milk, half-and-half, vanilla, and 1/8 teaspoon hazelnut extract in a clean jar.
  2. Shake well and taste in coffee before adding more hazelnut extract a few drops at a time.
  3. Label this jar clearly, refrigerate it, and shake before each use.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Hazelnut extract is strong, so begin with the smaller amount.
  • If anyone has a tree-nut allergy, check the extract label and avoid cross-contact with other creamer jars.
Machine note: Add homemade creamer to the finished drink and froth plain milk separately. Never pour creamer into a coffee maker's water reservoir.

Joni's Notes

This jar belongs to the kids. Joni's French vanilla remains unbothered. 😂

Storage & Make-Ahead

Keep tightly covered on a cold refrigerator shelf and use within 7 days or by the earliest expiration date of the dairy ingredients. Shake before each use. Freezing is not recommended because the texture may separate.
