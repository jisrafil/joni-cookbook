Chicken Parmesan

Chicken · Italian · Main Dish

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 35 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 cup marinara sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Pound or slice the chicken breasts to an even thickness so they cook evenly.
  3. Set up three shallow dishes: flour in the first, beaten eggs in the second, and breadcrumbs mixed with Parmesan, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in the third.
  4. Coat each chicken breast in flour, then egg, then the breadcrumb mixture, pressing gently so the coating sticks.
  5. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat and brown the chicken on both sides until golden.
  6. Transfer to a baking dish. Spoon marinara over each piece and top with mozzarella.
  7. Bake about 20 minutes, or until the chicken reaches 165°F internally and the cheese is melted.
  8. Serve hot with pasta or salad.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Keep the chicken an even thickness; one giant end and one paper-thin end will never finish at the same time.
  • Let excess egg drip off before breading so the coating stays crisp instead of gummy.
  • Do not drown the chicken in marinara before baking. Enough sauce to flavor it, not enough to erase the crust.

Joni's Notes

Crispy chicken, sauce, cheese. Nobody needs a sales pitch for this one.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate for up to 3–4 days. Reheat in the oven or air fryer for the best crust. Freeze cooked portions for up to 2 months.
