Cheese-Only Quiche

Breakfast · Brunch · Quiche

Prep: 45 min   Cook: 40 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 2–4 tablespoons ice water
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup half-and-half
  • 1 cup shredded cheese

Directions

  1. For the crust, combine the flour and salt. Cut in the cold butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  2. Add ice water 1 tablespoon at a time until the dough holds together. Shape into a disk, wrap, and chill 30 minutes.
  3. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Roll the dough and fit it into a 9-inch pie plate.
  4. Whisk the eggs and half-and-half until smooth, then stir in the cheese.
  5. Pour into the crust and bake 35–40 minutes, until the center is set and the top is golden. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.

JP Tips & Technique

  • A mix of sharp cheddar and a good melting cheese gives both flavor and creaminess.
  • Keep the butter cold while making the crust for the flakiest result.
  • Shield the crust edge with foil if it browns too quickly.

Joni's Notes

No vegetables hiding, no meat competing—just creamy eggs, cheese, and a flaky crust doing their job.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered up to 4 days. Freeze cooled slices up to 2 months and reheat at 325°F.
