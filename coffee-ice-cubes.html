Coffee Ice Cubes

Coffee Bases, Syrups & Cold Foam
Prep: 5 min   Yield: 1 standard ice tray

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cold-brew coffee or cooled brewed coffee

Directions

  1. Pour the cold coffee into a clean ice-cube tray.
  2. Freeze until solid, then transfer the cubes to a labeled freezer container.
  3. Use in iced coffee or replace part of the regular ice in a blended frappé.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Do not pour hot coffee into a plastic ice tray; cool it first.

Joni's Notes

Because watery iced coffee is disrespectful. 😂

Storage & Make-Ahead

Keep frozen in an airtight container for up to 2 months for best flavor.
