Extra-Creamy Vanilla Coffee Creamer

Homemade Coffee Creamers
Prep: 5 min   Yield: About 1 cup

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup half-and-half
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tiny drop butter extract
  • 1 tiny pinch fine sea salt

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a clean 1-cup jar.
  2. Shake until completely blended.
  3. Chill for 1 hour and shake again before adding to coffee.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Butter extract is strong; use only one tiny drop for a soft, custardy finish.
Machine note: Add homemade creamer to the finished drink and froth plain milk separately. Never pour creamer into a coffee maker's water reservoir.

Joni's Notes

French vanilla's extra-rich cousin.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Keep tightly covered on a cold refrigerator shelf and use within 7 days or by the earliest expiration date of the dairy ingredients. Shake before each use. Freezing is not recommended because the texture may separate.
