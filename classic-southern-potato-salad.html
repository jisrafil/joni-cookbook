Classic Southern Potato Salad

Side Dish · Potatoes · Summer · Picnic

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 15 min   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds russet potatoes
  • 1 cup Duke's mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 cup sweet pickle relish
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped celery
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped red onion
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Paprika, for garnish

Directions

  1. Peel and dice potatoes into bite-sized pieces. Place in a large pot and cover with water.
  2. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, reduce heat, and simmer 10–15 minutes until fork-tender. Drain and cool.
  3. Mix Duke's mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, and pickle relish in a large bowl. Stir in eggs, celery, and red onion.
  4. Gently fold in cooled potatoes and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour; overnight is even better.
  6. Sprinkle with paprika before serving.

Joni's Notes

Duke's. Not 'mayonnaise.' Duke's mayonnaise. Add crumbled bacon if the spirit moves you. Yukon Gold potatoes make it creamier.

Storage & Leftovers

Keep refrigerated and return it to the fridge promptly after serving. Best within 3–4 days. Do not freeze; the texture will suffer and nobody deserves sad potato salad.
