Chick-fil-A Nuggets (Copycat)

Chicken · Main Dish · Copycat

Prep: 45 min   Cook: 15 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts, cubed
  • 1/2 cup dill pickle juice
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Oil, for frying

Directions

  1. Place the cubed chicken in the dill pickle juice and marinate for about 30 minutes.
  2. Whisk the egg and milk together in one bowl.
  3. Combine the flour, powdered sugar, paprika, salt, and black pepper in another bowl.
  4. Drain the chicken. Dip pieces in the egg mixture, then dredge well in the seasoned flour.
  5. Heat frying oil to about 350°F.
  6. Fry the nuggets in batches for about 3–4 minutes per side, until golden and the chicken reaches 165°F internally.
  7. Drain on a rack or paper towels and serve hot.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Keep the chicken pieces similar in size so they finish cooking together.
  • Do not overcrowd the frying oil or the temperature will drop and the coating can get greasy.
  • A wire rack is better than a pile of paper towels if you want the coating to stay crisp.

Joni's Notes

Tiny chicken pieces somehow disappear twice as fast as full-size chicken. Plan accordingly.

Storage & Leftovers

Best fresh. Refrigerate cooked nuggets up to 3 days and reheat in an oven or air fryer. The source recipe also works well for freezing dredged, uncooked nuggets up to 1 month.
