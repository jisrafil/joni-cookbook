Collard Greens

Side Dish · Greens · Southern

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 60 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 pound collard greens, cleaned and chopped
  • 1 smoked turkey leg or 4 slices bacon
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Dash red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon oil, as needed for sautéing

Directions

  1. Wash the collard greens thoroughly, remove tough stems, and chop the leaves.
  2. Heat a little oil in a large pot. Sauté the onion and garlic until softened.
  3. Add the greens, chicken broth, smoked turkey or bacon, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if using.
  4. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook 45–60 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the greens are tender.
  5. If using smoked turkey, remove the meat from the bone, shred it, and stir it back into the greens before serving.
  6. Taste the pot liquor and adjust the seasoning before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Collards can hide grit like it is their full-time job. Wash them more than once if needed.
  • Start with less salt when using smoked meat because the broth and meat may already be salty.
  • Do not throw away that pot liquor. That is flavor, not leftover water.

Joni's Notes

The greens are good. The pot liquor might be even better.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate up to 5 days. Collard greens freeze very well for up to 2 months.
