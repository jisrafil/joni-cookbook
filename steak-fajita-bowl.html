Steak Fajita Bowl

Main Dish · Steak · Tex-Mex · Bowl

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 10 min   Yield: 2 bowls

Ingredients

  • 1 lb skirt or flank steak
  • 1 tablespoon fajita seasoning
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • 2 cups seasoned rice
  • 1 cup roasted potatoes

Directions

  1. Season the steak all over with fajita seasoning.
  2. Heat the oil in a hot skillet. Cook the steak about 3–4 minutes per side, adjusting for thickness and desired doneness.
  3. Transfer the steak to a cutting board and let it rest before slicing against the grain.
  4. In the same skillet, sauté the sliced bell pepper and onion until tender and lightly browned.
  5. Divide the seasoned rice and roasted potatoes between two bowls.
  6. Top with the sliced steak and sautéed vegetables and serve warm.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Skirt and flank steak both benefit from slicing against the grain; it makes a big difference in tenderness.
  • Let the steak rest before slicing so the juices stay in the meat instead of decorating the cutting board.
  • Use the same skillet for the vegetables after the steak—the browned bits are free flavor.

Joni's Notes

Rice AND potatoes in the bowl because sometimes choosing one starch is simply unnecessary.

Storage & Leftovers

Best fresh. Refrigerate leftovers for 2–3 days. Rice and cooked steak can be frozen separately for up to 2 months.
