Plain Bagels

Bread · Breakfast · Bagels

Prep: 2 hr   Cook: 22 min   Yield: 8 bagels

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups warm water (about 110°F)
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast (1 packet)
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 3 1/2 cups bread flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 2 quarts water, for water bath
  • 2 tablespoons honey, for water bath

Directions

  1. Mix the warm water, sugar, and yeast in a large bowl. Let stand 5–10 minutes, until foamy.
  2. Add the bread flour and salt. Mix until a shaggy dough forms, then knead by hand or mixer for 8–10 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
  3. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise about 1 hour, until doubled.
  4. Punch down the dough and divide into 8 equal pieces. Shape each into a smooth ball, poke a hole through the center, and gently stretch into a bagel shape.
  5. Cover and rest 10–15 minutes while you prepare the water bath and preheat the oven to 425°F.
  6. Bring the 2 quarts water and honey to a boil. Boil the bagels for 1 minute per side.
  7. Transfer to a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake 20–22 minutes, until golden brown.
  8. Cool before slicing.

JP Tips & Technique

  • The dough should feel firm and smooth rather than soft and sticky; bagel dough is intentionally stiffer than sandwich-bread dough.
  • Shape the center holes larger than you think you need because the dough expands during boiling and baking.
  • Do not skip the water bath. That quick boil is what gives bagels their chewy crust instead of turning them into round bread with identity issues. 😂

Joni's Notes

The plain bagel is the little black dress of breakfast. Toast it, schmear it, sandwich it—everybody knows what to do.

Storage & Leftovers

Store airtight at room temperature up to 2 days, refrigerate up to 5 days, or freeze up to 3 months. Slice before freezing for easier toasting.
