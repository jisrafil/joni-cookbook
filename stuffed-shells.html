Stuffed Shells

Pasta · Italian · Main Dish

Prep: 25 min   Cook: 50 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 18 jumbo pasta shells
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 container (15 oz) ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the shells according to package directions until al dente. Drain and cool enough to handle.
  3. Brown the ground beef with onion and garlic. Drain excess fat.
  4. Stir in marinara sauce, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Mix ricotta cheese and egg together in a bowl.
  6. Spoon ricotta filling into each shell and arrange the shells in a baking dish.
  7. Pour the meat sauce over the shells and top with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  8. Cover and bake for 30 minutes. Uncover and bake 10 more minutes, until bubbly.
  9. Let stand briefly before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Cook a few extra shells because one or two always decide they no longer want to participate in dinner and tear. 😂
  • Do not overfill the shells; they are easier to arrange and serve when the filling stays inside.
  • A piping bag or zip-top bag with the corner snipped makes filling shells much faster.

Joni's Notes

Same comfort-food family as lasagna, just wearing a different outfit.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered for up to 3–4 days. Stuffed shells freeze well before or after baking for up to 2–3 months.
