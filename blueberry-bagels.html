Blueberry Bagels

Bread · Breakfast · Bagels

Prep: 2 hr   Cook: 22 min   Yield: 8 bagels

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups warm water (about 110°F)
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast (1 packet)
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 3 1/2 cups bread flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 cup fresh or dried blueberries
  • 2 quarts water, for water bath
  • 2 tablespoons honey, for water bath

Directions

  1. Mix the warm water, sugar, and yeast and let stand 5–10 minutes, until foamy.
  2. Add the bread flour and salt. Knead 8–10 minutes, until smooth.
  3. Gently knead in the blueberries, trying not to crush them excessively.
  4. Place in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise about 1 hour, until doubled.
  5. Punch down and divide into 8 balls. Shape into bagels by making and stretching a center hole.
  6. Rest the shaped bagels 10–15 minutes while the oven heats to 425°F and the water bath comes to a boil.
  7. Boil each bagel 1 minute per side in the honey water.
  8. Transfer to a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake 20–22 minutes, until golden.

JP Tips & Technique

  • If using fresh blueberries, work gently so they do not turn the whole dough into purple paste.
  • Dried blueberries are easier to knead in and give a more predictable dough; fresh berries give juicier pockets.
  • If the dough becomes sticky from burst berries, use only a light dusting of flour rather than burying it in extra flour.

Joni's Notes

Blueberry bagels do not require permission to become breakfast and snack in the same day.

Storage & Leftovers

Store airtight at room temperature up to 2 days, refrigerate up to 5 days, or freeze up to 3 months.
