Panera-Style Cinnamon Crunch Bagels

Bread · Breakfast · Bagels

Prep: 1 hr 45 min   Cook: 25 min   Yield: 8 bagels

Ingredients

  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 1 tablespoon instant yeast
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1 tablespoon dark brown sugar
  • 1 1/3 cups warm water
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar, for topping
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon, for topping
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted, for topping

Directions

  1. Mix the bread flour, yeast, salt, and brown sugar in a large bowl. Gradually add the warm water and knead about 10 minutes, until smooth.
  2. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise about 1 hour, until doubled.
  3. Divide the dough into 8 pieces. Shape each piece into a bagel and let rest 15 minutes.
  4. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and boil each bagel for 1 minute per side. Drain well and place on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  5. Combine the granulated sugar, cinnamon, and melted butter until crumbly. Sprinkle generously over the bagels.
  6. Bake 20–25 minutes, until golden brown. Cool slightly before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Keep the dough firm rather than sticky; add only a little extra water if dry flour remains.
  • Press the cinnamon mixture on gently so more of that crunchy topping stays put.
  • The topping will be hot and soft straight from the oven but crisps as the bagels cool.

Joni's Notes

Sweet, chewy, cinnamon-crunchy, and absolutely worth the sticky baking sheet.

Storage & Leftovers

Store airtight at room temperature up to 2 days or refrigerate up to 5 days. Freeze up to 3 months; slice first for easy toasting.
