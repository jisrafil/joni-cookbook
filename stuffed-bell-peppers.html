Stuffed Bell Peppers

Main Dish · Comfort Food

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 35 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 large bell peppers, halved and seeds removed
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a baking dish.
  2. Brown the ground beef with the onion and garlic in a skillet. Drain excess fat.
  3. Stir in the cooked rice, diced tomatoes, salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  4. Arrange the pepper halves cut-side up in the baking dish and divide the filling among them.
  5. Cover and bake for 25 minutes.
  6. Uncover, top with cheddar cheese, and bake about 10 minutes more, until the peppers are tender and the cheese is melted.
  7. Let stand a few minutes before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Choose peppers that sit fairly level in the baking dish, or use pepper halves as written so nobody is fighting a falling pepper at dinner.
  • Do not pack the filling too tightly; spoon it in loosely.
  • If you like softer peppers, blanch the halves for 2–3 minutes before filling.

Joni's Notes

Meat, rice, vegetables, cheese—all minding their business in one edible bowl.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered for up to 3–4 days. Reheat in the oven or microwave. Freeze cooked peppers for up to 2 months.
