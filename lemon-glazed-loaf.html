Lemon Glazed Loaf

Baking · Dessert · Loaf · Citrus

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 55 min   Yield: 1 loaf

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon lemon zest
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar, for glaze
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice, for glaze

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a loaf pan.
  2. Cream the softened butter and sugar until light. Beat in the eggs, lemon juice, lemon zest, and milk.
  3. Add the flour, baking powder, and salt and stir only until combined.
  4. Spread the batter evenly in the prepared loaf pan.
  5. Bake 50–55 minutes, until golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  6. Cool the loaf before glazing.
  7. Whisk the powdered sugar and lemon juice together and drizzle over the cooled loaf.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Zest the lemon before juicing it. Trying to zest a squeezed lemon half is unnecessary kitchen suffering.
  • Do not glaze a hot loaf unless you want the glaze to disappear into it.
  • Let the loaf cool about 10–15 minutes in the pan before moving it to a rack so it has time to set.

Joni's Notes

Bright, sweet, lemony, and extremely compatible with coffee.

Storage & Leftovers

Store tightly covered at room temperature for 2–3 days or refrigerate up to 1 week. Freeze the loaf without glaze for up to 2 months.
