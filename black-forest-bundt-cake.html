Black Forest Bundt Cake

Dessert · Cake · Chocolate

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 45 min   Yield: 12 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 box devil's food cake mix
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 can (21 ounces) cherry pie filling
  • 1 teaspoon almond extract, optional
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream, for sauce
  • 1/2 cup semisweet chocolate chips, for sauce
  • 1 tablespoon butter, for sauce
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract, for sauce
  • Whipped cream, chocolate shavings, or extra cherries, optional

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a Bundt pan thoroughly.
  2. Combine the cake mix, eggs, oil, water, and almond extract. Mix until mostly smooth.
  3. Gently fold in the cherry pie filling without breaking up the cherries too much. Pour into the prepared pan and smooth the top.
  4. Bake 40–45 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the cake portion comes out clean. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then invert onto a rack and cool completely.
  5. For the sauce, heat the cream just to a simmer. Remove from the heat and stir in chocolate chips, butter, and vanilla until smooth and glossy.
  6. Cool the sauce slightly, then drizzle it over the cake. Garnish as desired.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Grease every curve of the Bundt pan so the cake releases cleanly.
  • Fold the cherries gently; those whole cherry pockets are part of the reward.
  • Wait until the cake is fully cool before glazing or the chocolate sauce will run straight off.

Joni's Notes

Chocolate and cherries already knew they belonged together. The Bundt pan just made it official.

Storage & Leftovers

Store covered at room temperature up to 2 days or refrigerate up to 5 days. Freeze unglazed cake slices up to 3 months.
