Traditional Salisbury Steak

Beef · Comfort Food · Main Dish

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 30 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds ground beef
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced (for gravy)
  • 8 ounces mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard powder

Directions

  1. Mix ground beef, breadcrumbs, chopped onion, egg, salt, pepper, and garlic. Shape into four oval patties.
  2. Cook patties in a large skillet over medium heat for about 5 minutes per side, until browned. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same skillet, sauté sliced onion and mushrooms until softened.
  4. Whisk beef broth, cornstarch, and water together, then pour into the skillet.
  5. Stir in ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, and mustard powder. Simmer until thickened.
  6. Return patties to the skillet, spoon gravy over them, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Serve with plenty of gravy.

Joni's Notes

Serve with mashed potatoes or buttered egg noodles. A beef-and-pork blend works too; add cayenne if you want a little attitude.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Handle the patties gently so they stay tender rather than dense.
  • Brown the patties well before making the gravy; those browned bits add flavor.
  • If the gravy gets too thick, loosen it with a splash of broth. If too thin, simmer it uncovered a little longer.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate patties with gravy for up to 3–4 days. Reheat gently on the stovetop or covered in the microwave. Freezes well with the gravy.
