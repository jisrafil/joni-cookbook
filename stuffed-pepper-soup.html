Stuffed Pepper Soup

Soup · Comfort Food

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 35 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 bell peppers, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 cup cooked white rice

Directions

  1. Brown the ground beef in a large pot over medium heat, breaking it into small pieces as it cooks. Drain excess fat.
  2. Add the onion, bell peppers, and garlic. Sauté until the vegetables begin to soften.
  3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, beef broth, salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for about 25 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cooked rice and simmer 5 minutes more.
  6. Taste, adjust seasoning, and serve hot.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Cut the peppers into fairly even pieces so they soften at the same rate.
  • Add the rice near the end so it does not absorb all the broth and turn the soup too thick.
  • If you expect leftovers, you can store the rice separately and add it to each bowl when reheating.

Joni's Notes

All the comfort of stuffed peppers without having to convince the peppers to stand up straight in a baking dish. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate up to 3–4 days. For best leftovers, store rice separately when possible. Freeze the soup base without rice for up to 2–3 months.
