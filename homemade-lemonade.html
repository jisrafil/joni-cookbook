Homemade Lemonade

Drink · Refreshing

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 0 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh lemon juice (about 4–5 lemons)
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 5 cups cold water
  • Ice cubes
  • Lemon slices, for garnish

Directions

  1. Whisk the fresh lemon juice and sugar together in a pitcher until the sugar is mostly dissolved.
  2. Add the cold water and stir well.
  3. Taste and adjust with a little more sugar, lemon juice, or water if needed.
  4. Chill until cold. Serve over ice and garnish with lemon slices.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Roll lemons firmly on the counter before cutting to help release more juice.
  • If the sugar does not want to dissolve, mix it first with a small amount of warm water, then add the lemon juice and remaining cold water.
  • Taste before serving—lemons vary, and lemonade has no respect for exact mathematics.

Joni's Notes

Cold lemonade tastes like somebody opened the back door and summer walked in.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered for up to 5–7 days. Stir before serving.
