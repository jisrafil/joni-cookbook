Brisket

BBQ · Beef · Main Dish

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 6 hr   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 5 pounds beef brisket
  • 2 tablespoons salt
  • 2 tablespoons black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce (optional)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 275°F (135°C).
  2. Mix salt, black pepper, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and cayenne, then rub the mixture over the brisket.
  3. Place brisket in a roasting pan and pour the beef broth and apple cider vinegar around it.
  4. Cover the pan very tightly with foil and roast for 5–6 hours, until the brisket is fork-tender.
  5. Remove from the oven and let rest at least 20 minutes before slicing.
  6. Slice against the grain and serve with the pan juices or BBQ sauce if desired.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Keep the pan tightly covered during the long roast so the brisket stays moist.
  • Tenderness matters more than the clock. If it still fights the fork, give it more time.
  • Always slice brisket against the grain; slicing with the grain can make tender meat seem chewy.

Joni's Notes

This is patience food. Put it in the oven, mind your business for several hours, and let the brisket do what brisket does.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate brisket with some of its juices for up to 3–4 days. Freeze sliced or portioned brisket with juices for up to 2–3 months.
