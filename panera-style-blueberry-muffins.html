Panera-Style Blueberry Muffins

Baking · Breakfast · Muffins · Copycat

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 30 min   Yield: 12 muffins

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1 1/2 cups blueberries
  • 2 tablespoons coarse sugar, for topping

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F and line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl.
  3. In a separate bowl, cream the softened butter and granulated sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then mix in the vanilla.
  5. Add the flour mixture alternately with the milk, mixing only until combined.
  6. Gently fold in the blueberries.
  7. Divide the batter among the muffin cups and sprinkle the tops with coarse sugar.
  8. Bake 25–30 minutes, until golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Do not overmix once the flour goes in; muffins get tough when the batter is worked too much.
  • Tossing very juicy blueberries with a teaspoon of the measured flour can help keep them distributed.
  • Fill the cups fairly generously for the bakery-style tops.

Joni's Notes

Bakery muffins at home means nobody has to stand in line before coffee. That's already a win.

Storage & Leftovers

Store airtight at room temperature up to 3 days or refrigerate up to 1 week. Freeze well for up to 2 months.
