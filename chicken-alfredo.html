Chicken Alfredo

Chicken · Pasta · Comfort Food

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 25 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 pound chicken tenders, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 box bow-tie pasta or fettuccine
  • 2 cups broccoli florets, fresh or frozen
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 4 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning, optional
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to package directions, adding the broccoli during the last 2–3 minutes. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Cook 5–7 minutes, until golden and cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
  3. In the same skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Stir in the cream cheese until smooth.
  4. Gradually whisk in the heavy cream and milk. Simmer 2–3 minutes, then stir in Parmesan and optional Italian seasoning. Cook until smooth and creamy.
  5. Return the chicken, pasta, and broccoli to the skillet. Toss to coat and warm through. Loosen with reserved pasta water if needed, then adjust the seasoning.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Soften the cream cheese so it melts smoothly instead of leaving little white lumps.
  • Grate Parmesan finely and keep the sauce below a hard boil.
  • Save pasta water before draining—it is the easiest way to loosen Alfredo sauce without watering down the flavor.

Joni's Notes

Chicken, pasta, broccoli, and enough creamy Alfredo sauce to make everybody stop talking for a minute.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate in an airtight container up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a splash of milk. Freezing is not recommended because the dairy sauce may separate.
