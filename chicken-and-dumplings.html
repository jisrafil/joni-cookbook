Chicken and Dumplings

Chicken · Comfort Food · Stew

Prep: 25 min   Cook: 2 hr 20 min   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, about 4–5 pounds
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 3 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 6 cups water
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

Directions

  1. Combine chicken, onion, carrots, celery, garlic, bay leaves, salt, pepper, and water in a large pot. Bring to a boil, then simmer about 2 hours until tender.
  2. Remove chicken. Discard skin and bones, shred the meat, and return it to the pot.
  3. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl. In another bowl, whisk milk and oil. Stir wet ingredients into dry ingredients until a soft dough forms.
  4. Drop spoonfuls of dough into the simmering stew.
  5. Cover and cook for 15 minutes, until dumplings are puffed and cooked through.

Joni's Notes

Chicken broth instead of water gives even more flavor. Peas or corn can join the party if you want them.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate up to 3–4 days. Dumplings will continue absorbing broth, so add a splash of broth when reheating. Freeze the chicken stew base best; fresh dumplings are better after thawing.
