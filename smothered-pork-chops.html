Smothered Pork Chops

Main Dish · Pork · Gravy · Skillet

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 40 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in pork chops
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour, for dredging
  • 1 teaspoon seasoning salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 2 tablespoons flour, reserved or additional, for gravy

Directions

  1. Pat the pork chops dry. Season with seasoning salt and black pepper, then dredge lightly in flour and shake off the excess.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the pork chops 3–4 minutes per side. Remove to a plate.
  3. Reduce heat to medium and cook the sliced onion in the pan drippings until softened.
  4. Stir in 2 tablespoons flour and cook about 1 minute to remove the raw flour taste.
  5. Gradually whisk in the chicken broth, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the skillet.
  6. Return the pork chops to the skillet and spoon gravy over them.
  7. Cover and simmer about 25 minutes, or until the chops are tender and cooked through.
  8. Taste the gravy and adjust seasoning before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Shake off excess dredging flour; too much flour can burn in the skillet.
  • Add broth gradually while stirring so the gravy stays smooth.
  • If the gravy gets too thick, loosen it with a splash of broth. If too thin, simmer uncovered for a few minutes.

Joni's Notes

If there is gravy left on the plate, somebody forgot the mashed potatoes or rice.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate pork chops in the gravy for up to 3–4 days. Reheat gently. Freeze with gravy for up to 2 months.
