Chicken Pot Pie

Chicken · Comfort Food · Main Dish

Prep: 25 min   Cook: 35 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, cubed
  • 2 pie crusts, homemade or refrigerated
  • 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables, such as peas, carrots, and corn
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 cup milk
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme or rosemary, optional

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Fit one crust into a 9-inch pie plate.
  2. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and sauté until softened.
  3. Stir in the flour and cook 1–2 minutes. Gradually whisk in the broth and milk and cook until thickened.
  4. Stir in the chicken and frozen vegetables. Season with salt, pepper, and optional herbs.
  5. Pour the filling into the bottom crust. Add the top crust, crimp the edges, and cut several steam vents.
  6. Bake 30–35 minutes, until the crust is deep golden and the filling is bubbling. Rest 10–15 minutes before slicing.

JP Tips & Technique

  • The filling should be thick before it goes into the crust; thin filling makes a soggy pie.
  • Place the pie plate on a sheet pan to catch any enthusiastic bubbling.
  • Turkey works beautifully here after the holidays.

Joni's Notes

A whole comfort-food meal tucked under a flaky crust. That is efficient love.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered up to 4 days. Reheat at 350°F until hot. Freeze the assembled unbaked pie up to 3 months; bake from frozen, allowing extra time.
