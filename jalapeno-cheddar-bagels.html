Jalapeño Cheddar Bagels

Bread · Breakfast · Bagels · Savory

Prep: 2 hr   Cook: 22 min   Yield: 8 bagels

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups warm water (about 110°F)
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast (1 packet)
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 3 1/2 cups bread flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1/3 cup diced pickled jalapeños, patted dry
  • 3/4 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese, divided
  • 2 quarts water, for water bath
  • 2 tablespoons honey, for water bath

Directions

  1. Mix the warm water, sugar, and yeast and let stand 5–10 minutes, until foamy.
  2. Add bread flour and salt and knead 8–10 minutes, until smooth.
  3. Gently knead in the dried jalapeños and 1/2 cup of the cheddar.
  4. Place dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise about 1 hour, until doubled.
  5. Punch down and divide into 8 equal pieces. Shape into bagels and rest 10–15 minutes.
  6. Preheat oven to 425°F. Bring water and honey to a boil.
  7. Boil each bagel 1 minute per side and transfer to a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  8. Sprinkle with the reserved 1/4 cup cheddar and bake 20–22 minutes, until golden and bubbly.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Pat the jalapeños very dry; extra pickle liquid can make the dough sticky.
  • Use freshly shredded cheddar when possible because it melts better than heavily coated pre-shredded cheese.
  • Any cheese that lands on the parchment and turns crispy belongs to the cook. Those are the rules.

Joni's Notes

A breakfast bagel with a little attitude. 🌶️

Storage & Leftovers

Store airtight at room temperature up to 2 days, refrigerate up to 5 days, or freeze up to 3 months.
