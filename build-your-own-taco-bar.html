Build-Your-Own Taco Bar

Mexican · Beef · Chicken · DIY · Party

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 15 min   Yield: 6 servings

Taco night the way it should be: everybody gets what they want, nobody complains, and there are enough toppings to turn it into a taco, a bowl, or a salad.

Ingredients

Taco Base

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin (for chicken)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder (for chicken)
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt (for chicken)
  • 8 small flour tortillas

Toppings

  • 4 cups shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese or Mexican blend
  • 1 can refried beans
  • 1/2 cup diced red onion
  • 1/2 cup black beans, drained and rinsed (optional)
  • 1/2 cup corn (optional)
  • 1/4 cup sliced black olives (optional)
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1/4 cup ranch dressing (optional)
  • 1/2 cup guacamole (optional)
  • 2 cups crushed tortilla chips (for salad option)

Pico de Gallo

  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup red onion, finely diced
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 jalapeno, finely diced (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Sides

  • 1 bag Doritos, Regular
  • 1 bag Doritos, Spicy Chili
  • 1 pack soft small tortillas

Directions

  1. Cook the Beef: Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat, breaking it up as it cooks. Drain excess grease, add taco seasoning and water, and simmer about 5 minutes until thickened.
  2. Prepare the Chicken: If using chicken, toss the shredded cooked chicken with cumin, garlic powder, and salt. Warm gently so it stays moist.
  3. Make the Pico de Gallo: Combine Roma tomatoes, red onion, cilantro, jalapeno if using, lime juice, and salt. Let it sit about 10 minutes so the flavors can get acquainted.
  4. Set Up the Bar: Warm the tortillas. Arrange the beef, chicken, toppings, pico de gallo, chips, and sides in separate bowls so everyone can build tacos, taco salads, or bowls.
  5. Serve: Set it all out and let everybody handle their own plate. Season until your ancestors are content.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Keep hot ingredients hot and cold toppings cold until serving.
  • Warm tortillas just before serving so they stay soft and flexible.
  • If making this for a crowd, prep the pico and toppings ahead, then cook the meat last.

Storage & Leftovers

Store meats, toppings, pico, and tortillas separately. Refrigerate cooked meat and pico promptly and use within 3–4 days. Reheat the meat gently; keep lettuce and other cold toppings cold.
