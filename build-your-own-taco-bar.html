Build-Your-Own Taco Bar

Prep: 20 minutes | Cook: 15 minutes | Total: 35 minutes | Serves: 6

Ingredients

Taco Base

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin (for chicken)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder (for chicken)
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt (for chicken)
  • 8 small flour tortillas

Toppings

  • 4 cups shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese or Mexican blend
  • 1 can refried beans
  • 1/2 cup diced red onion
  • 1/2 cup black beans, drained and rinsed (optional)
  • 1/2 cup corn (optional)
  • 1/4 cup sliced black olives (optional)
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1/4 cup ranch dressing (optional)
  • 1/2 cup guacamole (optional)
  • 2 cups crushed tortilla chips (for salad option)

Pico de Gallo

  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup red onion, finely diced
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 jalapeno, finely diced (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Sides

  • 1 bag Doritos, Regular
  • 1 bag Doritos, Spicy Chili
  • 1 pack soft small tortillas

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook the Beef: In a skillet over medium heat, cook ground beef until browned. Drain excess grease, then add taco seasoning and water. Simmer for 5 minutes until thickened.
  2. Prepare the Chicken: If using chicken, shred cooked chicken and toss with cumin, garlic powder, and salt.
  3. Make the Pico de Gallo: In a bowl, mix diced tomatoes, red onion, cilantro, jalapeno if using, lime juice, and salt. Let sit for 10 minutes.
  4. Set Up the Taco Bar: Warm tortillas and set out the meat, toppings, pico de gallo, salad ingredients, and sides so everyone can build a soft taco or assemble a taco salad.

JP Notes

Keep the taco bar flexible and let everybody build their own. Season until your ancestors are content. Share & serve with love. JP.
