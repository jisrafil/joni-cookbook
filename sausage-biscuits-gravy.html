Sausage Biscuits & Gravy

Breakfast · Southern · Comfort Food

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 25 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, cold and cubed
  • 3/4 cup whole milk
  • 1 pound pork sausage
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour (for gravy)
  • 2 cups whole milk (for gravy)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt together.
  2. Cut in cold butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs. Stir in milk until just combined.
  3. Roll out dough, cut biscuits, and bake 12–15 minutes until golden.
  4. Cook sausage in a skillet over medium heat until browned.
  5. Sprinkle flour over sausage, stir, and cook for 1 minute.
  6. Slowly add milk, stirring constantly, and simmer until thickened. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper.
  7. Split warm biscuits and ladle sausage gravy over the top.

Joni's Notes

A pinch of cayenne gives the gravy a little kick. Buttermilk makes excellent biscuits. This is not a breakfast for pretending you're only going to eat one biscuit.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Keep the butter cold and work it into the flour just until you have coarse crumbs. Warm butter means flatter biscuits.
  • Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and pat or gently roll it about 3/4 to 1 inch thick.
  • Fold the dough over itself a few times for flaky layers, but do not overwork it.
  • Cut straight down with a floured biscuit cutter; twisting can seal the edges and limit the rise.
  • Keep a little extra flour nearby for dusting the counter, rolling pin, and cutter.
  • If the gravy gets too thick, whisk in a splash of milk. If it is too thin, let it simmer a little longer.

Storage & Leftovers

Store biscuits and gravy separately in the refrigerator for up to 3–4 days. Reheat gravy gently with a splash of milk. Biscuits can be frozen separately.
