Chicken Salad

Chicken · Salad · Lunch

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 0 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked chicken, shredded or diced
  • 1/2 cup Duke's mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup celery, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup red grapes, halved, optional
  • 1/4 cup walnuts or almonds, chopped, optional
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Combine the chicken, Duke’s mayonnaise, celery, and lemon juice in a large bowl.
  2. Fold in the grapes and nuts, if using.
  3. Season with salt and black pepper. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour so the flavors blend.
  4. Serve on sandwiches, in lettuce cups, or with crackers.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Start with chilled chicken so the salad stays fresh and the mayonnaise does not loosen.
  • Cut the chicken and add-ins to a similar size for the best texture in every bite.
  • Taste again after chilling; cold foods sometimes need one final pinch of seasoning.

Joni's Notes

Creamy, crunchy, and flexible enough for sandwiches, crackers, lettuce cups, or a fork straight from the bowl.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate promptly in an airtight container and use within 3–4 days. Do not freeze; the mayonnaise and fresh add-ins separate after thawing.
