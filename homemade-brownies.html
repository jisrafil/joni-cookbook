Homemade Brownies

Dessert · Chocolate · Baking

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 30 min   Yield: 12 brownies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 1/4 cups sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • Butter or nonstick spray, for greasing the pan

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease an 8- or 9-inch square baking pan or line it with parchment.
  2. Whisk the melted butter and sugar together. Add the eggs and vanilla and mix until smooth.
  3. Add the flour, cocoa powder, salt, and baking powder.
  4. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet just until no dry streaks remain.
  5. Spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan.
  6. Bake 25–30 minutes, until the edges are set and a toothpick in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs rather than wet batter.
  7. Cool before cutting.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Do not overmix once the flour goes in; that can make brownies cakier and tougher.
  • For cleaner slices, let the brownies cool fully before cutting.
  • A toothpick with a few moist crumbs is what you want. Bone-dry toothpick means we have gone too far.

Joni's Notes

If somebody cuts the center square before the brownies cool, I am not responsible for the family dispute that follows. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Store tightly covered at room temperature for 3–4 days or refrigerate up to a week. Freeze individual brownies for up to 2–3 months.
