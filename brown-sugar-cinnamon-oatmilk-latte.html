Brown Sugar Cinnamon Oatmilk Latte

Hot Coffee & Lattes
Prep: 5 min   Yield: 1 drink

Ingredients

  • 1 concentrated coffee shot
  • 1 tablespoon dark brown sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • Oat milk filled to the LATTE line of the frother

Directions

  1. Place the brown sugar, vanilla, and cinnamon in a mug.
  2. Brew the concentrated coffee shot over the mixture and stir until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Hot-froth the oat milk and pour it over the coffee.

JP Tips & Technique

  • For an iced version, use cold foam and pour the finished drink over ice.

Joni's Notes

Warm, cozy, and just sweet enough without hiding the coffee.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Best made fresh. Refrigerate leftover coffee separately for up to 4 days.
