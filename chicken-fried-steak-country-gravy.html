Chicken Fried Steak with Country Gravy

Beef · Southern · Comfort Food

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 25 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 cube steaks
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 large egg
  • Vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour, for gravy
  • 2 cups milk, for gravy
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste, for gravy

Directions

  1. In a shallow bowl, combine 1 cup flour, salt, black pepper, paprika, and garlic powder. In a second bowl, whisk the buttermilk and egg.
  2. Dredge each steak in seasoned flour, dip in the buttermilk mixture, and coat once more in flour. Press the coating on well.
  3. Heat about 1/4 inch of vegetable oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the steaks until golden brown and cooked through, about 3–4 minutes per side. Transfer to a rack or paper towels.
  4. Carefully pour off the oil, leaving about 2 tablespoons of drippings in the skillet. Stir in the 1/4 cup flour and cook for 1 minute.
  5. Slowly whisk in the milk. Cook, whisking frequently, until the gravy is smooth and thickened. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Spoon the country gravy over the hot steaks and serve.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Let the breaded steaks rest 5–10 minutes before frying so the coating adheres better.
  • Keep the oil hot enough to sizzle steadily, but reduce the heat if the coating browns before the meat cooks.
  • Add the milk gradually and scrape up the browned bits—that is where the gravy earns its name.

Joni's Notes

Crispy steak, creamy gravy, and no apology anywhere on the plate.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate steak and gravy separately up to 3 days. Reheat steak in a 375°F oven or air fryer to restore crispness; warm gravy gently with a splash of milk. Freeze the cooked steak up to 2 months.
