Lasagna

Pasta · Italian · Main Dish

Prep: 25 min   Cook: 50 min   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 container (15 oz) ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions until just al dente. Drain and set aside.
  3. Brown the ground beef with onion and garlic in a skillet. Drain excess fat.
  4. Stir in marinara sauce, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Mix the ricotta cheese and egg together in a bowl.
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish. Add noodles, ricotta mixture, and more sauce. Repeat the layers, ending with sauce.
  7. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes. Uncover and bake about 10 more minutes, until hot and bubbly.
  9. Let stand for 10 minutes before cutting.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Slightly undercook the noodles because they continue cooking in the oven.
  • Coat the underside of the foil lightly with cooking spray or tent it so the melted cheese does not glue itself to the foil.
  • The 10-minute rest is not optional if you want slices instead of lasagna landslide.

Joni's Notes

Make a pan, feed everybody, and still somehow have somebody standing in the kitchen later cutting 'just a little piece.'

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered for up to 3–4 days. Lasagna freezes well baked or assembled before baking; wrap tightly and freeze for up to 2–3 months.
