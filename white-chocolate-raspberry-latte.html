White Chocolate Raspberry Latte

Hot Coffee & Lattes
Prep: 5 min   Yield: 1 drink

Ingredients

  • 1 prepared coffee shot, brewed or instant
  • 1 tablespoon white chocolate sauce
  • 1 teaspoon raspberry syrup
  • Milk filled to the LATTE line of the frother
  • Whipped cream, optional

Directions

  1. Add the white chocolate sauce and raspberry syrup to a mug.
  2. Pour the prepared brewed or instant coffee shot over the flavorings and stir well.
  3. Add hot-frothed milk and whipped cream if desired.

JP Tips & Technique

  • No concentrated-shot setting? Make the Instant Coffee Shot Substitute.
  • Start with 1 teaspoon raspberry syrup; it gets sweet quickly.

Joni's Notes

A pretty little dessert coffee without needing a coffee-shop run.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Best made fresh. Refrigerate sauces and milk according to their package directions.
