Grilled Corn on the Cob

BBQ · Side Dish

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 12 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 6 ears corn, husked
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika (optional)
  • Extra butter, for serving

Directions

  1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Brush the corn with melted butter and season with salt, black pepper, and smoked paprika if using.
  3. Grill for 10–12 minutes, turning every few minutes, until hot and lightly charred in spots.
  4. Serve immediately with extra butter.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Turn the corn regularly so one side does not burn while the rest stays pale.
  • A little char is flavor; completely black corn is evidence. 😂
  • Season again lightly after grilling if needed.

Joni's Notes

Summer side dish. No committee meeting required.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate leftover corn for up to 3–4 days. Reheat on the grill, in a skillet, or microwave. For easier leftovers, cut the kernels from the cob before refrigerating.
