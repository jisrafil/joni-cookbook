Iced Caramel Latte

Iced Coffee
Prep: 7 min   Yield: 1 drink

Ingredients

  • 1 concentrated coffee shot
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons caramel sauce
  • 6 ounces whole milk
  • 1 cup ice
  • Additional caramel sauce, for the cup

Directions

  1. Drizzle caramel sauce around the inside of a tumbler and fill it with ice.
  2. Brew the concentrated coffee shot.
  3. Cold-froth the milk with the caramel sauce, then pour it over the ice.
  4. Add the coffee and stir before drinking.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Cool the coffee shot for a few minutes if you want the ice to melt more slowly.

Joni's Notes

Caramel in the cup, caramel in the foam, and no drive-through line.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Assemble just before serving. Refrigerate the coffee shot separately for up to 4 days if making ahead.
