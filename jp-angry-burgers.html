JP's Angry Burgers

Beef · Burgers · Spicy

Prep: 30 min   Cook: 20 min   Yield: 6 burgers

Ingredients

  • 6 hamburger buns
  • 2 pounds ground beef, formed into 6 patties
  • 6 slices pepper jack cheese
  • 6 slices thick-cut bacon, cooked crisp
  • 1/2 cup breaded fried jalapeño slices
  • 6 cooked onion rings
  • 6 iceberg lettuce leaves
  • 6 tomato slices
  • 6 red onion slices
  • 6 pickle slices
  • 1/2 cup Duke's mayonnaise, for sauce
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup, for sauce
  • 1 teaspoon hot sauce, for sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper, for sauce
  • 1 teaspoon paprika, for sauce
  • Buttermilk, seasoned flour, and frying oil, as needed for jalapeños

Directions

  1. Stir together the Duke’s mayonnaise, ketchup, hot sauce, cayenne, and paprika. Refrigerate until needed.
  2. For fried jalapeños, slice fresh jalapeños into thin rings. Dip in buttermilk, dredge in seasoned flour, and fry in 350°F oil for 2–3 minutes, until crisp. Drain well.
  3. Grill or pan-fry the burger patties until they reach 160°F. Top each with pepper jack and let it melt. Toast the buns if desired.
  4. Layer lettuce, tomato, red onion, pickle, and a cheeseburger patty on each bottom bun.
  5. Top with bacon, fried jalapeños, and an onion ring. Spread angry sauce on the top bun and close the burger.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Make the sauce first so the flavors have time to wake up.
  • Wear gloves when slicing jalapeños and keep your hands away from your eyes.
  • Build these at the last minute so the onion rings and fried jalapeños stay crisp.

Joni's Notes

This burger woke up angry and chose pepper jack, bacon, fried jalapeños, and an onion ring. Respectfully, it has a point.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate cooked patties and toppings separately up to 3 days. The sauce keeps up to 5 days. Fried toppings are best fresh; re-crisp them in an air fryer or hot oven.
