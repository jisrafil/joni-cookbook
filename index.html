Joni's Cookbook ❤️

These recipes are more than measurements and directions. They are meals I’ve made, flavors I’ve loved, things my family has eaten around the table, and pieces of a life that kept moving even when I had to slow down.

This cookbook has been a long time coming. Some recipes were written down carefully, some were built from memory, some grew out of conversations, and some needed a little help finding their way back home. I’m gathering them here so they won’t be lost again — and so the people I love can keep making them long after the original scrap of paper, text file, or “a little of this and a little of that” is gone.

Season until your ancestors are content :)   Share & serve with love. JP
