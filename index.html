Why This Table Matters ♥

These are the meals I fed you, the dishes we gathered around, the things I learned to make by watching, tasting, remembering, and adding a little more until it was right.

Some were written down. Some lived only in my head. Some of you probably thought I was going to take them with me because apparently “a little bit” is not a recognized unit of measurement. 😂

So I'm writing them down.

When I think about our family and the years we spent around the table, I know we each carry our own memories of them. There were good times, hard times, tragedies, triumphs, growing pains, and everything in between.

But dinner? Dinner was good.

We said grace. We held hands. We talked. We ate.

Sometimes dinner was meatloaf. Salmon patties and fried potatoes. Salisbury steak. Taco salad. Spaghetti. They weren't fancy meals. Sometimes they were simply what I could make with what we had. But they fed my babies, and they gave us a place to come together.

Looking back, that's what matters to me. Not whether every meal was perfect. Not whether I measured everything correctly—because we all know I didn't. 😂

It's that we had our table. And now I want to make sure you have the recipes that went with it.

For my babies. For your babies. For whoever comes next.
Make them exactly like I did, or make them your own. Just keep feeding each other.

Love, Mom ❤️
