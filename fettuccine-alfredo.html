Fettuccine Alfredo

Pasta · Italian · Main Dish

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 15 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces fettuccine pasta
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg (optional)
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish

Directions

  1. Cook fettuccine according to package directions. Reserve a little pasta water, then drain.
  2. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Stir in heavy cream, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for about 3 minutes, stirring constantly.
  4. Reduce heat to low and stir in Parmesan until melted and smooth. Add nutmeg if using.
  5. Toss the hot fettuccine with the sauce. Add a splash of reserved pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.
  6. Serve immediately with parsley.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Use freshly grated Parmesan when possible; pre-shredded cheese can make the sauce less smooth.
  • Keep the heat low after adding Parmesan. High heat can make the sauce grainy or oily.
  • Alfredo waits for nobody. Have the table ready before you finish the sauce.

Joni's Notes

Cream, butter, Parmesan. We are not pretending this is salad. Enjoy your dinner. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3–4 days. Reheat gently over low heat with a splash of milk or cream. Alfredo is best fresh and is not ideal for freezing.
