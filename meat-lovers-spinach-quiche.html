Meat Lovers & Spinach Quiche

Breakfast · Brunch · Quiche

Prep: 45 min   Cook: 40 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 2–4 tablespoons ice water
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup half-and-half
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1/2 cup cooked sausage
  • 1/2 cup chopped ham
  • 1/2 cup cooked crumbled bacon
  • 1 cup fresh spinach, chopped

Directions

  1. Make the crust by combining flour and salt, cutting in cold butter, and adding ice water until the dough holds together. Shape into a disk and chill 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Roll the dough and fit it into a 9-inch pie plate.
  3. Whisk the eggs and half-and-half until smooth. Stir in the cheese, cooked meats, and chopped spinach.
  4. Pour into the crust and bake 35–40 minutes, until the center is just set and the top is golden.
  5. Rest 10–15 minutes before slicing.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Wilt the spinach briefly and squeeze it dry if it seems especially moist.
  • A pinch of garlic powder or red pepper flakes balances the rich meat and cheese.
  • Cool cooked fillings before combining them with the eggs.

Joni's Notes

All the meat-lovers goodness, plus spinach so we can look ourselves in the eye. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered up to 4 days. Freeze cooled slices up to 2 months and reheat at 325°F.
