Shrimp Egg Rolls

Appetizer · Seafood · Crispy

Prep: 25 min   Cook: 10 min   Yield: 8 egg rolls

Ingredients

  • 1/2 pound shrimp, peeled, cooked, and chopped
  • 1 cup coleslaw mix
  • 1/4 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 8 egg roll wrappers
  • Oil, for frying
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water, for sealing

Directions

  1. Combine the chopped shrimp, coleslaw mix, carrots, and garlic powder in a bowl.
  2. Place one egg roll wrapper in a diamond shape. Add filling near the lower third, leaving enough room to fold.
  3. Fold the bottom corner over the filling, fold in both sides, and roll tightly toward the top.
  4. Brush the final corner with the cornstarch-water mixture to seal.
  5. Heat frying oil to about 350°F.
  6. Fry egg rolls in batches for 2–3 minutes per side, until crisp and golden brown.
  7. Drain on a rack or paper towels and serve hot.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Do not overfill the wrappers or they will split while frying.
  • Keep unused wrappers covered with a barely damp towel so they do not dry out while you work.
  • A cooling rack keeps fried egg rolls crisper than piling them directly on paper towels.

Joni's Notes

Make more than eight if people are standing in the kitchen while you fry them. That's all I'm saying. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Best fresh. Refrigerate cooked leftovers up to 2 days and reheat in an air fryer or oven. Unfried egg rolls can be refrigerated up to 24 hours or frozen before frying.
