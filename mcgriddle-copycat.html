McGriddle (Copycat)

Breakfast · Sandwich · Copycat

Prep: 25 min   Cook: 15 min   Yield: 2 sandwiches

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 slices American cheese
  • 2 sausage patties
  • 4 pancake rounds (3–4 inch)
  • Maple syrup, or maple crystals for pancake batter
  • Butter or oil, for cooking

Directions

  1. Prepare four small pancake rounds, about 3–4 inches wide, and keep them warm.
  2. Cook the sausage patties until browned and cooked through.
  3. Cook the eggs in butter or oil, shaping them roughly to fit the pancake rounds.
  4. Place one sausage patty on a pancake, top with egg and American cheese, then add the second pancake.
  5. Add maple syrup between the layers if using syrup rather than maple crystals.
  6. Warm the assembled sandwiches briefly in a skillet or microwave if needed and serve.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Keep the pancakes small and fairly thick so the sandwich holds together.
  • For meal prep, let every component cool before wrapping so steam does not make the pancakes soggy.
  • If using maple syrup, use a light hand inside the sandwich. You want maple flavor, not breakfast running down your wrist.

Joni's Notes

Storage & Leftovers

Best fresh. Wrap cooled sandwiches individually and refrigerate up to 3 days or freeze up to 1 month. Reheat until hot throughout.
