Spinach & Cheese Quiche

Breakfast · Brunch · Quiche

Prep: 45 min   Cook: 40 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 2–4 tablespoons ice water
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup half-and-half
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 cup fresh spinach, chopped

Directions

  1. Make the crust by combining flour and salt, cutting in the butter, and adding ice water 1 tablespoon at a time until the dough holds together. Shape into a disk and chill 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Roll the dough and fit it into a 9-inch pie plate.
  3. Whisk the eggs and half-and-half until smooth. Stir in the cheese and spinach.
  4. Pour into the crust and bake 35–40 minutes, until the center is just set and the top is golden.
  5. Rest 10–15 minutes before slicing.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Squeeze excess moisture from the spinach so the custard stays creamy, not watery.
  • Sharp cheddar is dependable; adding feta or Gruyère makes it a little fancier.
  • Do not overbake—the filling should remain tender.

Joni's Notes

A reliable brunch quiche that feels special without requiring a special occasion.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered up to 4 days. Freeze cooled slices up to 2 months and reheat gently.
