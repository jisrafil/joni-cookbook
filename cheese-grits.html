Cheese Grits

Breakfast · Side Dish · Southern

Prep: 5 min   Cook: 25 min   Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup stone-ground grits
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream (optional)
  • Black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan. Slowly stir in the grits and salt.
  2. Reduce heat to low and cook for 20–25 minutes, stirring regularly so the grits stay creamy and do not stick.
  3. Stir in butter, cheddar cheese, and heavy cream if using.
  4. Season with black pepper and serve warm.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Add the grits slowly while stirring to help prevent lumps.
  • Stone-ground grits need time; keep the heat low and let them get creamy instead of rushing them.
  • If the grits tighten up before serving, stir in a splash of water, milk, or cream.

Joni's Notes

These can be breakfast, a side dish, or the foundation for shrimp and grits. We don't believe in limiting grits around here.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate in a covered container for up to 3–4 days. Reheat gently with a splash of water, milk, or cream to loosen them.
