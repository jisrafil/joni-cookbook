Stuffed Cabbage Leaves

Main Dish · Comfort Food

Prep: 30 min   Cook: 50 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 12 large cabbage leaves
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Blanch the cabbage leaves 1–2 minutes, just until flexible. Drain and cool enough to handle.
  3. Brown the ground beef with the onion in a skillet. Drain excess fat.
  4. Stir in cooked rice, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and Worcestershire sauce.
  5. Trim any very thick stem ridge from the bottom of each cabbage leaf so it rolls easily.
  6. Place filling near the stem end of each leaf. Fold the sides inward and roll snugly.
  7. Place rolls seam-side down in a baking dish and pour tomato sauce over the top.
  8. Cover and bake about 45 minutes. Rest a few minutes before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Blanch only long enough to make the leaves flexible; overcooked cabbage tears more easily.
  • Keep the filling portions similar so the rolls cook and serve evenly.
  • Seam-side down is free insurance against your cabbage rolls unrolling themselves in the oven.

Joni's Notes

A little more work than a casserole, but the kind of dinner that looks like you meant business.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered for up to 3–4 days. Cabbage rolls freeze well for up to 2–3 months; freeze with sauce to help keep them moist.
