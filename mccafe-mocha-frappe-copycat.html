McCafé Mocha Frappé Copycat

Frappés & Frozen Drinks · Coffeehouse Copycats
Prep: 7 min   Yield: 1 large drink

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup strong coffee base, cold-brew concentrate or instant
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 2 tablespoons chocolate syrup
  • 1 tablespoon simple syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tiny pinch fine sea salt
  • 1 1/2 cups ice
  • 2 tablespoons half-and-half, optional
  • Whipped cream, for topping
  • Additional chocolate syrup, for drizzling

Directions

  1. Drizzle chocolate syrup around the inside of a tall glass.
  2. For an instant base, dissolve 2 teaspoons NESCAFÉ Gold Espresso Intense or Café Bustelo Instant Espresso, or 2 1/2 teaspoons Folgers Classic Roast Instant, in 2 tablespoons hot water. Add enough cold water to make 1/2 cup and cool.
  3. Add the cold-brew concentrate or prepared instant base, milk, chocolate syrup, simple syrup, vanilla, salt, ice, and half-and-half if using to a blender.
  4. Blend for 30 to 45 seconds, until completely smooth and slushy.
  5. Pour into the prepared glass and top with whipped cream and chocolate syrup.

JP Tips & Technique

  • For ready-to-drink cold brew, use 3/4 cup cold brew and reduce the milk to 1/4 cup.
  • For a thicker texture, add 2 tablespoons vanilla ice cream or 1/8 teaspoon xanthan gum.
  • Replacing some regular ice with coffee ice cubes prevents a watery frappé.

Joni's Notes

The drive-through favorite, except now the blender knows your name.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Serve immediately. The blended drink separates and thins as it sits.
