Sweet Potato Pie

Dessert · Southern · Pie

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 60 min   Yield: 1 9-inch pie

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 2 cups mashed sweet potatoes
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg
  • Pinch of salt

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Place the pie crust in a 9-inch pie plate if it is not already fitted.
  2. Beat the mashed sweet potatoes and softened butter until smooth.
  3. Add the sugar, evaporated milk, eggs, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Mix until evenly combined and creamy.
  4. Pour the filling into the unbaked pie crust and smooth the top.
  5. Bake 55–65 minutes, until the edges are set and the center has only a slight jiggle.
  6. Cool completely before slicing.

JP Tips & Technique

  • For the smoothest filling, mash or puree the sweet potatoes while they are still warm, then cool before combining with the eggs.
  • Do not overbake just because the center looks slightly soft; it continues setting as it cools.
  • Set the pie plate on a sheet pan for easier handling and to catch any enthusiastic filling.

Joni's Notes

This pie needs enough time to cool before everybody starts circling the kitchen asking if it's ready yet.

Storage & Leftovers

Cover and refrigerate for up to 4 days. Wrap well and freeze whole or in slices for up to 2 months.
