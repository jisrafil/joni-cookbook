Beef Stew

Soup · Stew · Comfort Food

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 2 hr 10 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds beef stew meat, cubed
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 2 large potatoes, diced
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 1 teaspoon rosemary
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup flour, for thickening
  • Water, as needed for flour slurry

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large heavy pot over medium-high heat.
  2. Working in batches if needed, brown the beef on all sides. Remove and set aside.
  3. Sauté the onion and garlic until softened, then stir in the tomato paste and cook briefly.
  4. Return the beef to the pot. Add broth, potatoes, carrots, celery, thyme, and rosemary.
  5. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Cover partially and cook 1 1/2–2 hours, until the beef is tender.
  6. For a thicker stew, whisk the flour with enough cool water to make a smooth slurry, then stir it into the simmering stew.
  7. Simmer about 10 more minutes. Season with salt and pepper and serve.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Pat the beef dry before browning so it actually sears instead of steaming.
  • Do not crowd the pot when browning; work in batches if necessary.
  • Add flour slurry gradually. You can always thicken it more, but nobody wants stew that accidentally became gravy cement.

Joni's Notes

This is one of those pots that tastes even better after everybody has gone to bed and you sneak another spoonful. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Cool and refrigerate for up to 3–4 days. Beef stew freezes well for up to 2–3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator and reheat gently.
