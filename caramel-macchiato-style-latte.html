Caramel Macchiato-Style Latte

Hot Coffee & Lattes · Coffeehouse Copycats
Prep: 5 min   Yield: 1 drink

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon vanilla syrup
  • Milk filled to the LATTE line of the frother
  • 1 prepared coffee shot, brewed or instant
  • 1 tablespoon caramel sauce

Directions

  1. Add the vanilla syrup to a mug.
  2. Hot-froth the milk and pour it over the vanilla syrup.
  3. Prepare a brewed or instant coffee shot, pour it through the foam, and finish with caramel sauce.

JP Tips & Technique

  • No concentrated-shot setting? Make the Instant Coffee Shot Substitute.
  • Pouring the coffee through the milk creates the layered macchiato-style finish.

Joni's Notes

Sweet vanilla underneath, bold coffee in the middle, and caramel over the top.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Best assembled fresh. Refrigerate opened milk and caramel sauce according to their package directions.

Share & serve with love. JP ❤️
