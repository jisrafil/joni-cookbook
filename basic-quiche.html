Basic Quiche

Breakfast · Brunch · Quiche

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 40 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked all-butter pie crust
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded cheese, such as cheddar, feta, goat cheese, or Gruyère
  • Up to 2 cups cooked add-ins, optional
  • Extra cheese, chopped herbs, hollandaise, or black pepper, optional toppings

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Place the unbaked crust in a 9-inch pie plate.
  2. Whisk together the eggs, milk, cream, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  3. Stir in the shredded cheese and any fully cooked, well-drained add-ins.
  4. Pour the filling into the crust. Bake 35–40 minutes, until the center is just set and the top is lightly golden.
  5. Let cool 10–15 minutes before slicing. Add optional toppings before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Cook watery vegetables before adding them and squeeze spinach very dry.
  • The center should have a slight jiggle when removed; it continues setting as it rests.
  • Keep total add-ins to about 2 cups so the custard can hold everything together.

Joni's Notes

This is the foundation recipe: learn the custard, then clean out the refrigerator with confidence.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered up to 4 days. Freeze cooled individual slices up to 2 months. Reheat at 325°F until warmed through.
