Chicken & Rice Bake

Chicken · Casserole · Comfort Food · Main Dish

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 55 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese (optional)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a baking dish, mix the uncooked rice, chicken broth, and cream of mushroom soup until evenly combined.
  3. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika.
  4. Nestle the chicken on top of the rice mixture. Cover the dish tightly with foil.
  5. Bake for 45 minutes. Check that the rice is tender and the chicken reaches 165°F internally.
  6. Remove the foil, add cheddar cheese if using, and bake about 10 minutes more.
  7. Let stand 5–10 minutes before serving so the rice can finish absorbing the liquid.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Use a baking dish that can be covered tightly; trapped steam is what cooks the rice.
  • Do not keep lifting the foil to check it. Every peek lets out the steam the rice needs.
  • If your chicken breasts are very thick, pound them slightly or cut them into more even portions.

Joni's Notes

One pan. Meat and rice together. Exactly the kind of dinner a busy day deserves.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate in a covered container for up to 3–4 days. Reheat until hot throughout. Freeze individual portions for up to 2 months.
