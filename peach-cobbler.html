Peach Cobbler

Dessert · Southern

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 45 min   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 cups sliced peaches (fresh or canned)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Vanilla ice cream, for serving (optional)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix the peaches, sugar, and cinnamon together and set aside.
  3. Pour melted butter into a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  4. Whisk flour, milk, baking powder, and salt together until combined.
  5. Pour the batter over the butter. Do not stir.
  6. Spoon the peaches evenly over the batter. Again, do not stir.
  7. Bake 40–45 minutes, until the top is golden and the filling is bubbling around the edges.
  8. Let cool slightly and serve warm, preferably with vanilla ice cream if we're making good decisions. 😂

JP Tips & Technique

  • If using canned peaches, drain excess syrup unless you specifically want a looser, sweeter cobbler.
  • Do not stir the layers after they go into the baking dish—the batter rises around the fruit as it bakes.
  • Place the baking dish on a sheet pan if it is very full; bubbling peach juice has no respect for a clean oven.

Joni's Notes

Warm peach cobbler and vanilla ice cream is one of those combinations that did not need improving.

Storage & Leftovers

Cover and refrigerate leftovers for up to 3–4 days. Reheat portions in the oven or microwave. Cobbler can be frozen for up to 2 months, though the topping is best fresh.
