BBQ Chicken Drumsticks

BBQ · Chicken · Main Dish

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 45 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 6 chicken drumsticks
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with foil for easier cleanup.
  2. Mix the olive oil, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Pat the drumsticks dry and coat them evenly with the spice mixture.
  4. Arrange on the baking sheet with a little space between each piece. Bake 35 minutes, turning once halfway through.
  5. Brush generously with BBQ sauce and return to the oven for about 10 minutes, until sticky and the chicken reaches at least 165°F internally.
  6. Rest a few minutes before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Patting the chicken dry helps it brown instead of steam.
  • Wait until near the end to add BBQ sauce; the sugar in the sauce can burn during a long bake.
  • For extra caramelization, broil for 1–2 minutes at the end and do not walk away from the oven.

Joni's Notes

Sticky fingers are part of the recipe.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate cooked drumsticks for up to 3–4 days. Reheat in the oven or air fryer. Freeze for up to 2 months.
