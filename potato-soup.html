Potato Soup

Soup · Comfort Food · Budget-Friendly

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 30 min   Yield: 4–6 servings

Ingredients

  • 4–5 large potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 cup milk or half-and-half
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese (optional topping)
  • 1/4 cup chopped green onions (optional topping)
  • 1/4 cup cooked bacon bits (optional topping)

Directions

  1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and garlic and sauté until the onion is translucent.
  2. Add the diced potatoes and broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 15–20 minutes, until the potatoes are fork-tender.
  3. Mash some of the potatoes directly in the pot for a thicker soup. For a smoother soup, use an immersion blender on part of the mixture.
  4. Stir in milk or half-and-half. Season with salt and black pepper and simmer gently a few more minutes to heat through.
  5. Ladle into bowls and add cheese, green onions, or bacon if desired.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Cut potatoes into similar-size pieces so they finish cooking together.
  • Do not vigorously boil the soup after adding dairy; a gentle simmer keeps it smoother.
  • Yukon Gold potatoes give the soup a naturally creamy texture. A little smoked paprika is good here too.

Joni's Notes

Cheap, filling, warm, and somebody will absolutely go back for another bowl.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Reheat gently, adding a little broth or milk if the soup thickens. For best freezer texture, freeze the soup before adding the dairy.
