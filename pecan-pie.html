Pecan Pie

Dessert · Pie

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 55 min   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 1 cup light corn syrup
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups pecans, chopped or halved

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Whisk the corn syrup, sugar, eggs, melted butter, and vanilla together until smooth.
  3. Stir in the pecans until evenly distributed.
  4. Pour the filling into the unbaked 9-inch pie crust.
  5. Bake for 50–55 minutes, until the edges are set and the center has only a slight jiggle.
  6. Cool completely before slicing so the filling can finish setting.

JP Tips & Technique

  • If the crust edges brown too quickly, shield them loosely with foil during the last part of baking.
  • A slight jiggle in the center is fine; a liquid wave is not. The filling firms as it cools.
  • Put the pie on a sheet pan before baking. Sticky pecan-pie filling on the bottom of the oven is not part of the recipe.

Joni's Notes

This pie needs to cool before you cut it. I know. Nobody likes waiting around a pecan pie either. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Cover and refrigerate for up to 4 days. Bring slices toward room temperature before serving if you prefer a softer filling. Freeze tightly wrapped for up to 2 months.
