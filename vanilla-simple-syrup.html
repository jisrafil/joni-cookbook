Vanilla Simple Syrup

Coffee Bases, Syrups & Cold Foam
Prep: 5 min   Cook: 5 min   Yield: About 1 1/2 cups

Ingredients

  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Combine the sugar and water in a small saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Stir just until the sugar dissolves; do not boil for an extended time.
  3. Remove from the heat, cool for 5 minutes, and stir in the vanilla.
  4. Cool completely before transferring to a clean covered bottle or jar.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Add the vanilla after heating so its flavor stays noticeable.

Joni's Notes

One bottle, several coffees, and no mystery ingredients.

Storage & Make-Ahead

Refrigerate in a clean covered container for up to 3 weeks. Discard if it becomes cloudy, develops mold, or smells fermented.
