Classic Potato Salad

Side DishPotatoesSummerPicnic

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds potatoes (Yukon Gold or red potatoes)
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup yellow mustard
  • 2 teaspoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 cup sweet pickle relish
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 3 celery stalks, finely chopped
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Paprika (optional, for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Cook the Potatoes: Wash and cut potatoes into bite-sized pieces. Boil in salted water until tender but not falling apart, about 10-15 minutes. Drain and let cool.
  2. Prepare the Dressing: In a large bowl, mix mayonnaise, mustard, apple cider vinegar, and sweet pickle relish until well combined. Joni's note: mix and mash some of the potatoes into the dressing.
  3. Combine Ingredients: Add the cooled potatoes, diced onion, celery, and chopped eggs to the dressing.
  4. Season: Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. For extra tang, add more mustard or vinegar as desired.
  5. Chill: Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour to allow flavors to meld.
  6. Serve: Before serving, sprinkle with paprika.

Notes

  • Best served chilled for maximum flavor.
  • Adjust mustard or vinegar to taste.
  • Great for barbecues, potlucks, and picnics.
