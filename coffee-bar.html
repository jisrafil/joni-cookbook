Build the Same Coffee Bar in AnyList

Import each webpage as its own recipe. Do not save this whole collection as one recipe.

STEP 1Create a main recipe collection named Coffee Bar.
STEP 2Create Hot, Iced, Frappés, Copycats, Bases, Creamers, and Seasonal subcollections.
STEP 3Open an individual recipe page, share or import its web address into AnyList, and assign its collections.
STEP 4Scale each chosen drink or creamer before adding its ingredients to your shopping list.

Coffee Bar Restock List

  • Ground or instant coffee — restock at 1/4 container
  • Sweetened condensed milk — keep one can
  • Half-and-half — restock at 1/2 carton
  • Whole or oat milk — keep one carton
  • Vanilla and favorite flavor extracts
  • Caramel and chocolate sauce
  • Whipped cream — keep one can
  • Cold-brew concentrate — make at one quart
  • Ice and coffee filters
Shopping for one creamer: Import that flavored recipe and add all of its ingredients. Shopping for a sampler: Add the base recipe once, then add only the flavoring ingredients from each small jar you choose.
Keep ingredient names identical. Use “whole milk,” “half-and-half,” “sweetened condensed milk,” “chocolate syrup,” “caramel sauce,” “vanilla syrup,” and “prepared coffee shot” the same way in every recipe so AnyList can combine their quantities correctly.
