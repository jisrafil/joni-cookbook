Buffalo Chicken Dip – Classic

Appetizer · Dip · Chicken

Prep: 10 min   Cook: 10 min   Yield: 12 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked shredded chicken
  • 8 oz cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup buffalo sauce
  • 1/2 cup ranch dressing
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Tortilla chips or celery, for serving

Directions

  1. Place the cream cheese, buffalo sauce, and ranch dressing in a skillet or saucepan over low heat.
  2. Stir gently until the cream cheese melts and the mixture is smooth.
  3. Add the shredded chicken and cheddar cheese.
  4. Continue stirring over low heat until the chicken is hot and the cheese is melted.
  5. Serve warm with tortilla chips or celery.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Softened cream cheese melts faster and more smoothly.
  • Keep the heat low so the dairy stays creamy rather than separating.
  • If the dip thickens too much while sitting, stir in a spoonful of ranch or a small splash of milk.

Joni's Notes

Put this on the table with chips and suddenly everybody is standing around the dip pretending they are not waiting for dinner.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate covered for 3–4 days. Reheat gently on the stove or in short microwave intervals, stirring between each.
