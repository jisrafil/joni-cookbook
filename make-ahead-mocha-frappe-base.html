Make-Ahead Mocha Frappé Base

Frappés & Frozen Drinks · Coffee Bases, Syrups & Cold Foam
Prep: 5 min   Yield: 4 frappés

Ingredients

  • 2 cups strong coffee base, cold-brew concentrate or instant
  • 1/2 cup chocolate syrup
  • 1/4 cup simple syrup
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt

Directions

  1. For an instant base, dissolve 8 teaspoons NESCAFÉ Gold Espresso Intense or Café Bustelo Instant Espresso, or 10 teaspoons Folgers Classic Roast Instant, in 1/2 cup hot water. Add enough cold water to make 2 cups and cool.
  2. Combine the cold-brew concentrate or prepared instant base with the remaining ingredients in a covered jar or bottle and shake thoroughly.
  3. For one frappé, blend 2/3 cup base with 1/2 cup whole milk and 1 1/2 cups ice.
  4. Top with whipped cream and chocolate syrup.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Choose either cold-brew concentrate or the instant option; you do not need both.
  • Shake the refrigerated base before each use because the chocolate can settle.

Joni's Notes

Storage & Make-Ahead

Refrigerate the dairy-free base for up to 5 days. Add milk only when blending a drink.
