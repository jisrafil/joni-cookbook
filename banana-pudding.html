Banana Pudding

Dessert · Southern

Prep: 20 min   Cook: 0 min   Chill/Rest: 2 hr   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 box vanilla pudding mix
  • 2 cups cold milk
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3 cups whipped topping
  • 4 bananas, sliced
  • 1 box vanilla wafers

Directions

  1. Whisk the pudding mix, cold milk, sweetened condensed milk, and vanilla together until thickened.
  2. Fold in the whipped topping until smooth and fluffy.
  3. Layer vanilla wafers, sliced bananas, and pudding mixture in a serving dish.
  4. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used, finishing with pudding on top.
  5. Cover and chill for at least 2 hours before serving.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Use ripe bananas with good flavor, but avoid bananas so soft they turn the layers mushy immediately.
  • Slice the bananas as you assemble so they spend less time exposed to air.
  • The wafers soften as the pudding chills—that is a feature, not an emergency.

Joni's Notes

This is one of those desserts where somebody will ask who made it before they even finish the first bowl.

Storage & Leftovers

Keep covered and refrigerated. Best within 2–3 days because the bananas and wafers continue softening. Banana pudding is not freezer-friendly.
