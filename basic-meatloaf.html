Basic Meatloaf

BeefComfort FoodMain Dish

Ingredients

Meatloaf

  • 2 pounds ground beef
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme (optional)

Topping

  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon mustard (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the Oven: Preheat oven to 375F (190C). Grease a loaf pan or line it with parchment paper.
  2. Prepare the Meat Mixture: In a large bowl, combine ground beef, breadcrumbs, milk, eggs, onion, garlic, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper, and thyme if using. Mix until just combined; do not overmix.
  3. Shape and Top: Transfer the mixture into the prepared loaf pan and press lightly to shape. In a small bowl, mix the topping ingredients and spread evenly over the meatloaf.
  4. Bake: Bake for 50-60 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 160F (70C).
  5. Rest: Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.

Notes

  • Jarred garlic works fine.
  • Easily doubled.
