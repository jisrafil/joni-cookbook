Basic Meatloaf

Beef · Comfort Food · Main Dish

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 60 min   Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds ground beef
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme (optional)
  • 1/4 cup ketchup (for topping)
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar (for topping)
  • 1 teaspoon mustard (optional, for topping)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a loaf pan or line it with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, combine ground beef, breadcrumbs, milk, eggs, onion, garlic, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper, and thyme if using. Mix until just combined; do not overmix.
  3. Transfer the mixture to the prepared loaf pan and press lightly to shape.
  4. Mix the topping ketchup, brown sugar, and mustard if using, then spread evenly over the meatloaf.
  5. Bake for 50–60 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 160°F (71°C).
  6. Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

Joni's Notes

Jarred garlic works fine. Easily doubled.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days. Reheat covered so it stays moist. Slices can also be frozen for longer storage.
