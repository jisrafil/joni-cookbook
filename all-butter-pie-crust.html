All-Butter Pie Crust

Baking · Pie · Pastry

Prep: 30 min   Cook: 0 min   Chill/Rest: 2 hr   Yield: 2 crusts

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled
  • 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, chilled and cubed
  • 1/2 cup ice water, plus more as needed
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar, optional, replacing 1 tablespoon of the water

Directions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Cut in the cold butter with a pastry cutter or fork until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with pea-size pieces of butter still visible.
  3. Drizzle in the ice water about 2 tablespoons at a time, tossing gently until the dough begins to form clumps. Do not overwork it.
  4. Transfer to a lightly floured surface and gently bring the dough together into a ball.
  5. Divide into two equal portions, shape each into a flat disc, and wrap tightly.
  6. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before rolling.
  7. When ready to use, roll gently from the center outward on a lightly floured surface.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Cold butter is the point. Those small butter pieces create flaky layers when they hit the hot oven.
  • Stop adding water as soon as the dough holds together when squeezed; wet pie dough becomes tough pie crust.
  • Roll from the center outward and rotate the dough instead of sawing back and forth with the rolling pin.
  • The optional apple cider vinegar can help with tenderness without making the finished crust taste like vinegar.

Joni's Notes

This is one of those recipes where less messing with it gives you a better result. Do not overwork the dough.

Storage & Leftovers

Refrigerate wrapped dough discs up to 3 days. Freeze tightly wrapped for up to 3 months; thaw overnight in the refrigerator before rolling.
