Sweet Tea

Drink · Southern

Prep: 5 min   Cook: 10 min   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 8 cups water, divided
  • 3 black tea bags
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda (optional, for smoothness)
  • Lemon slices, for garnish

Directions

  1. Bring 4 cups of the water to a boil in a saucepan.
  2. Remove from heat, add the tea bags, and steep 5–10 minutes depending on how strong you like your tea.
  3. Remove the tea bags without squeezing them excessively. Stir in the sugar and baking soda if using until dissolved.
  4. Pour into a pitcher and add the remaining 4 cups cold water.
  5. Chill thoroughly and serve over ice with lemon slices if desired.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Do not boil the tea bags; boiling can make tea more bitter.
  • Stir the sugar in while the tea is hot so it dissolves completely.
  • Baking soda is optional—a tiny amount can soften some of the tea's bitterness.

Joni's Notes

The pitcher belongs in the refrigerator. Everybody knows where the glasses are. 😂

Storage & Leftovers

Keep refrigerated and use within about 5 days for best flavor.
