Chocolate Chess Pie

Dessert · Southern · Pie

Prep: 15 min   Cook: 40 min   Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 3 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Whipped cream, for serving (optional)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Whisk the sugar and cocoa powder together first to break up cocoa lumps.
  3. Add the eggs, melted butter, evaporated milk, vanilla, and salt. Whisk until smooth.
  4. Pour the filling into the unbaked pie crust.
  5. Bake 35–40 minutes, until the edges are set and the center has only a slight jiggle.
  6. Cool completely before slicing. Serve with whipped cream if desired.

JP Tips & Technique

  • Mixing sugar and cocoa before adding wet ingredients helps keep the filling smooth.
  • Do not wait for the center to look dry in the oven; the pie continues setting as it cools.
  • Let it cool fully before slicing unless you want chocolate chess soup with crust. 😂

Joni's Notes

Chocolate pie with Southern credentials. Enough said.

Storage & Leftovers

Cover and refrigerate for up to 4 days. Let slices sit briefly at room temperature before serving if you prefer a softer texture.
